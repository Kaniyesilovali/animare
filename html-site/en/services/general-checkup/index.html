Your pet can't tell you how they feel — but we can find out. In Gönyeli / Nicosia, regular check-up appointments are the most effective way to catch serious conditions before symptoms appear. Our veterinarian examines the heart, lungs, joints, skin, eyes, ears, and teeth from head to tail, referring to our in-house laboratory or radiology when needed. Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I bring my pet for a check-up?

Annual check-ups for healthy adults, every 6 months for seniors or pets with ongoing conditions. Puppies and kittens need more frequent visits in their first year.

What does a general check-up include?

A comprehensive physical examination of the heart, lungs, joints, skin, eyes, ears, and teeth. Blood tests or radiology are arranged where needed.
