Vaccination

The single most life-saving thing you can do for your pet often takes under five minutes. Vaccination protects against deadly diseases including parvovirus, distemper, and rabies. We offer all core and supplementary vaccines for dogs and cats, with a personalised schedule based on your pet's age, lifestyle, and health history. Cards are updated after every visit — and we track the next reminder for you.

Service Scope

  • Dog core vaccine (DHPPi + L)
  • Cat core vaccine (FVRCP + FeLV)
  • Rabies vaccine
  • Parvovirus and distemper protection
  • Annual booster reminders — we let you know when it's time

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I vaccinate my puppy or kitten?

Vaccination programmes typically begin at 6–8 weeks of age. Adult pets receive annual booster doses to maintain immunity.

Which vaccines are essential?

Core vaccines for dogs include DHPPi+L and rabies; for cats, FVRCP and FeLV form the core programme.

Are there side effects after vaccination?

Mild soreness or temporary lethargy is normal. If you notice a severe reaction, contact our clinic immediately.
