Frequently Asked Questions

What types of veterinary services does Animare provide?

We offer nine core services: general check-ups, vaccination, surgery, dental care, digital radiology, 24/7 emergency care, laboratory diagnostics, pet grooming, and nutrition consulting.

Do you treat both dogs and cats?

Yes, we care for both dogs and cats. Our services are tailored to the specific needs of each species, from puppies and kittens to senior pets.

Is there an emergency service available at weekends?

Our 24/7 emergency service operates every day of the week, including weekends and public holidays. Do not hesitate to call us in any urgent situation.
