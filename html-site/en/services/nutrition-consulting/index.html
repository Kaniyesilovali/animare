Nutrition Consulting

“Suitable for all breeds” on the bag rarely means it's actually right for your pet. Our veterinary team assesses your pet's life stage, breed, weight, and health history to build a feeding plan made for them — whether that's a growing puppy, an overweight adult, or a cat with kidney disease. Recommended N&D Farmina premium foods are available directly at the clinic.

The wrong food doesn't announce itself. It shows up months later as a dull coat, itching, loose stools, or extra weight. The right food goes unnoticed — because nothing goes wrong.

How the plan is built

The assessment starts with an examination. Your pet is weighed, given a body condition score, and checked for coat and skin condition; age, breed, neuter status, activity level, and any chronic conditions are recorded. We also ask what they eat now and how many treats — the arithmetic usually comes apart right there.

What you leave with is concrete: a daily amount in grams, a number of meals, and a specific product recommendation — not vague advice to “cut back a bit”, but a programme you can measure on a scale.

When nutrition becomes part of the treatment

Excess weight directly increases joint load, diabetes risk, and anaesthetic risk, and a controlled diet can reverse that. In chronic kidney disease, adjusting phosphorus and protein is one of the most effective ways to slow its progress.

For skin allergies, limited-ingredient or hydrolysed protein diets help track down the source of the itching. For urinary stones, diet is as decisive as medication in preventing recurrence. For joint problems, omega-3 formulas can reduce the need for painkillers.

Four mistakes we see most often

Measuring dry food by eye, not counting treats towards the daily calories, giving table scraps as “just a little”, and switching foods too often. A fifth is ignoring life stage: puppy food means excess calories for an adult cat, and energy needs drop after neutering. We review the plan once a year.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get nutrition advice for my pet?

Call us on +90 533 844 91 92 or use the contact page to book. Our vet assesses your pet during the consultation and prepares a personalised nutrition plan.

How do I know which food is right for my pet?

Our vet assesses age, breed, weight, and health status to build a personalised plan, recommending N&D Farmina premium foods or therapeutic diets where appropriate.

Are N&D Farmina products available at your clinic?

Yes, the premium N&D Farmina range recommended by our veterinarians is stocked at our clinic.

Can you create a weight management plan?

Yes. Our vet designs controlled diet programmes and schedules regular weigh-ins to measure progress.

What is the difference between therapeutic and regular pet food?

Therapeutic diets are formulated to specific medical requirements and used on veterinary advice. Regular food suits healthy animals but cannot address medical needs.

How should I switch my pet to a new food?

Spread it over 7–10 days: mix in about a quarter of the new food, then increase the proportion every few days. A sudden change can cause diarrhoea.
